“approved → feedback” is not a mistake and the drawer must not treat it as one. A buyer who approved a sample and then sent a further comment is the normal case; a machine that refused it would force somebody to record the second comment as a new request and lose the thread. “closed” is terminal from everywhere, which is why Close sits in the footer of every state rather than in a menu.
