Foundation

Warp and weft

The mark is two sets of threads crossing — the moment loose yarn becomes cloth. That's the whole product thesis: scattered inputs (a buyer's email, a supervisor's tally sheet, a bank advice) crossed into one fabric a factory can hold. Every design decision below traces back to it.

Structure over decoration

A merchandiser reads 400 rows a day. Alignment, tabular numerals and consistent row rhythm do more for them than any gradient. Ornament earns its place or leaves.

Amber means you

The brand colour is reserved for two things: the action you should take, and anything waiting on your judgment. It is never a status. That restraint is what makes it work.

Built for bad conditions

Glare, dust, a five-year-old shared PC, internet that drops mid-entry. If it only looks good on a MacBook in a quiet office, it isn't finished.

Identity

Logo

Two locked variants. The wordmark's crossing X is the only part that may be used alone — as the app icon, favicon, loading state, and the source of the thread motif.

Do
  • Keep clear space equal to the height of the "f" on all sides
  • Minimum width 96px on screen, 24mm in print
  • Place on ink #101725, near-black, white, or #EFF2F7 only
  • Use the X mark alone at 16–48px (favicon, app icon, avatars)
Don't
  • Recolour the wordmark, or make "ai" anything but #EAB308
  • Place it over photography, gradients, or busy fabric textures
  • Add glow, bevel, outline, or drop shadow
  • Stretch, condense, rotate, or rebuild it in a different typeface

Typography

Archivo · Inter · Anek Bangla

Archivo for display — an industrial grotesque with the flat terminals and tight fit of shipping-carton stencils and factory signage. Inter for the interface, because at 13px in a 40-column table nothing else stays this readable. Anek Bangla for Bengali, a variable family drawn to sit at the same weight and rhythm as Latin sans. JetBrains Mono for anything a human reads character by character: LC numbers, style codes, roll IDs.

Do
  • Tabular numerals on every figure that stacks in a column
  • Sentence case everywhere except mono labels

Don't
  • Go below 12px for anything a supervisor reads standing up
  • Use weight 700+ for body copy on dark — it blooms
  • Set Bengali in a Latin fallback; the conjuncts break
  • Hard-code strings — every label is an i18n key from day one
Signature

The thread motif

One borrowed element, used in exactly four places. It comes from the crossing strokes in the mark, so it belongs to this product and no other.

Thread rule

Separates a page header from its content. Amber at the top of a page, ink between sections. Never both on one screen.

Weave loader

Replaces every spinner. Threads interlacing while MARBIM works — and the honest signal that something is being made, not just fetched.

Voice

How the product talks

Two languages, one register: direct, specific, never chatty. English is written to be translated — no idioms, no wordplay in UI strings.

01

Name what the person controls, not how it's built. "Pending approvals," never "pending_changes queue." "Fabric in store," never "inventory sync status."

02

Errors say what happened and what to do. "Couldn't save — you're offline. Your entry is queued and will send when you reconnect." Not "Something went wrong."

03

Empty states are instructions. "No orders yet. Upload a buyer PO and MARBIM will draft one for you to check."

04

MARBIM never claims certainty it doesn't have. "I read 61,500 pcs, but the date was ambiguous — confirm 02 Oct?" beats a confident wrong answer every time.

05

Numbers carry their unit and currency, always. "$6.80 FOB/pc" and "৳ 12,400" — never a bare figure. Two currencies live in this product and mixing them costs real money.

Implementation

Space, depth, and the floor test

Scale

4px base. Steps: 4 · 8 · 12 · 16 · 24 · 32 · 48 · 64. Table rows 44px on desktop, 56px on floor devices. Radius: 8 controls · 12 inputs · 16 cards · 20 drawers.

Depth

Three surfaces only — canvas, surface, raised. Depth comes from a 1px border at 9% white, not shadow. Glass (blur(20px)) is reserved for drawers, modals, and the sticky top bar.

The floor test

Every screen a supervisor touches must pass all five, or it doesn't ship:

  • Readable at arm's length on a 1366×768 monitor
  • Touch targets ≥44px, reachable with one thumb
  • Entered data survives a dropped connection
  • Works with backdrop-filter disabled
  • Full task completable in Bengali
