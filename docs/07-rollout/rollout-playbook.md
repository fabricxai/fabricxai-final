# FabricXAI — Factory Rollout Playbook
### Department-by-department go-live with a smooth learning curve

**The rule:** never ask a department to use the system before the department upstream is feeding it data. Roll out in the direction the order flows.

## Timeline (per factory)

| Window | Who goes live | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Week 0 | Nobody (champion + us) | Pick the champion (senior merchandiser or commercial manager). Load masters: buyers, styles, lines, worker roster, wage gazette grades, defect codes, TNA template — MARBIM extracts from their existing files/photos. |
| Weeks 1–2 | Merchandising (2–3 people) | New inquiries flow through extraction→approve→quote. Champion back-enters in-flight orders so the order book is real. Value felt: no more retyping buyer emails. |
| Weeks 3–4 | Store + Commercial together | GRNs on the floor tablet; open LCs and UDs entered. Together, because bonded GRNs need UD refs. First "oh" moment: the live UD balance screen. |
| Weeks 5–8 | Floor, 2 lines → all | Hourly entry on 2 lines, 1-hour training, 2 weeks parallel with the paper board. When digital matches paper and supervisors stop reaching for the pen: +4 lines, then the floor. Cutting + QC inline join here. |
| Weeks 9–12 | Shipment, Finance, Owner | Shipments of system-born orders go out; packing validates real breakdowns. Owner dashboard LAST — only honest once fed. |
| Month 4+ | Payroll | Attendance import early (passive). One full month parallel vs their Excel, every net figure diffed, before anyone is paid from FabricXAI. Compliance + Maintenance slot anywhere after week 8. |

## Learning curve by role (design the training to these budgets)

| Role | Time to competent | Training format |
|---|---|---|
| Storekeeper, line supervisors | < 1 day | 1-hour hands-on at their device; laminated 1-page bn cheat sheet; parallel paper for trust |
| Merchandisers | 3–5 days | half-day workshop + champion shadowing; extraction pays them back day one |
| Commercial officer | 2–3 days | verify-the-math sessions (expandable calculations exist for exactly this) |
| Mechanics, QC inline | half a day | ticket queue / tap-grid walkthrough |
| HR | 3 days + parallel month | payroll run rehearsals on seed data first |
| Owner | 30 minutes | one evening walkthrough of feed → approve → ask on their own phone |

## The champion's real job (months 1–3)
Walk the floor, not configure software: watch entries happen, catch discipline drift early (missed hourly entries, GRNs batched at day-end), feed friction back weekly. Give them a standing 30-min weekly call with the dev side; their observations outrank any analytics.

## Adoption metrics that matter (review weekly)
Entry latency (hourly outputs entered within the hour: target >90% by week 8) · extraction approval-without-correction rate rising · paper artifacts retired (count them) · owner opens/week (>4 = the feed earns its place) · time-to-quote before vs after.

## Rollback dignity
If a department stalls, retreat one step without shame (back to parallel paper), fix the friction, re-advance. A forced march creates fake data; fake data kills the owner dashboard; a dead dashboard kills the sale. Patience at the floor level is the cheapest thing you can spend.
